Meet WD Brown.

Raised in inner-city New York, WD's path was anything but linear. He dropped out of high school, earned his GED on the third try, and had to rebuild — mentally, emotionally, spiritually — long before the results ever showed.

That journey shaped a message he shares with anyone ready to hear it, whether you're just starting out or already “successful”:

before the millions, you have to become whole.

Through what he calls the Focus Filter, WD teaches a practical way to cut through distractions, align your life, and build with intention instead of pressure. For three decades, he has walked beside people finding direction, rebuilding after setbacks, and chasing a version of success that finally feels aligned.

WD is also the founder of Vision Buildaz, the coaching and mentoring platform behind “You Ready? Let's Grow!”® — where his approach comes to life through 1:1 and group coaching.

He knows how to find the heart of a person and answer in a way that makes you see.

Audience member

His wisdom isn't handed down from a stage. It's shared person to person, wherever someone's ready for it.

I'm not an expert. I'm a practitioner. I'm growing with you.
